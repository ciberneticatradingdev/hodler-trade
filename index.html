Frequently asked

Everything you need to know about hodler.trade.

How does the loyalty score work?
Your loyalty score is calculated based on three factors: hold duration (how long you've held), consistency (no selling or reducing your position), and accumulation (buying more over time). The longer and stronger you hold, the higher your score climbs. Scores update every minute.
How often are rewards distributed?
Rewards are distributed every 15 minutes automatically. Each cycle, the top-scoring holders for each token receive their share of the reward pool. No claims needed — tokens land directly in your wallet.
Which tokens are supported?
hodler.trade works with any SPL token on Solana. Token creators can set up their token on the platform in under 2 minutes. If a token you hold is registered on hodler.trade, you're automatically eligible for rewards.
Do I need to stake or lock my tokens?
No. There's no staking, no lockups, and no smart contract interactions required. Simply hold the token in your wallet. That's it. Your tokens remain fully liquid and under your control at all times.
What happens if I sell some tokens?
Selling reduces your loyalty score. The scoring algorithm tracks consistency — wallets that hold steady or accumulate more are rewarded higher. If you sell a portion, your score adjusts downward proportionally. If you sell everything, your score resets to zero.
How do I know if rewards were sent?
All reward distributions are recorded on-chain on Solana. You can verify any distribution through Solscan or any Solana block explorer. The live activity feed on our platform also shows recent distributions in real time.
I'm a token creator. How do I set this up?
Setting up takes under 2 minutes. Connect your wallet, register your token, choose your reward token, set the distribution interval and scoring parameters, fund the reward pool, and you're live. A real-time dashboard lets you monitor distributions and holder behavior.
Is there a fee?
hodler.trade takes a small percentage fee on each reward distribution to sustain the platform. The exact fee structure is transparent and visible before you set up your token. There are no hidden charges.
